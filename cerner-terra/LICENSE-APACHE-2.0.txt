Terra Core was archived in 2024 with peer dependencies pinned to React 16.
The sources under src/vendor are copied and mechanically adapted for React 19
by scripts/vendor.mjs; component bodies are otherwise unmodified. Adaptations:
defaultProps on function components replaced by an equivalent wrapper,
react-intl and terra-theme-context replaced by local shims, and terra-icon
imports replaced by local stubs.
